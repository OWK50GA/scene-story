THE LAST SIGNAL

Written for SceneStory Continuity Testing.
Universe: The Last Signal
Project: The Last Signal (Canon Tier 1)
Story Unit: Film E — "The Last Signal"
In-Universe Period: Present Day, 2026
In-Universe Date Start: 2026


INT. MERIDIAN ARCHIVE — NIGHT

Rain lashes the windows of a small government archive.

ELENA VALE, 34, a careful intelligence archivist, unlocks a steel cabinet.
Inside sits a BLUE SIGNAL CASE.

Elena removes the case and places it on her desk.
She opens it. Inside: a compact radio transmitter.

ELENA
The last transmission was forty years ago.

She checks a handwritten inventory.

ELENA
Blue Signal Case. Archive storage. Locked.

Elena closes the case.
She places it in the locked cabinet.


EXT. MERIDIAN ARCHIVE — NIGHT

Elena exits the building.

Across the street, MARCUS REED, 40s, watches from beneath an umbrella.
He speaks quietly into a phone.

MARCUS
She found it.

A pause.

MARCUS
No. She doesn't know what it does.


INT. ELENA'S APARTMENT — MORNING

Elena studies photographs from the archive.
One photograph shows her father standing beside the Blue Signal Case.

On the back:

"NEVER TRANSMIT AFTER MIDNIGHT."

Elena writes the warning into her notebook.
She underlines it twice.


INT. MERIDIAN ARCHIVE — DAY

Elena returns to the archive. The cabinet is still locked.
She opens it.

The BLUE SIGNAL CASE IS GONE.

Elena freezes. She checks the inventory.

ELENA
That isn't possible.

Her supervisor, DAVID KLINE, enters.

DAVID
What happened?

ELENA
The case was here last night.

David examines the empty cabinet.

DAVID
Did you move it?

ELENA
No.


INT. ARCHIVE SECURITY ROOM — DAY

Elena and David review security footage.
At 2:13 A.M., Marcus enters the archive.
He walks directly to the cabinet.
He removes the Blue Signal Case.
The footage cuts out.

DAVID
Who is he?

ELENA
Marcus Reed. Former field operative.

Elena writes in her notebook:

CASE — POSSESSED BY MARCUS REED


INT. ABANDONED TRAIN STATION — EVENING

Marcus places the Blue Signal Case on an old wooden bench.
He opens it. The transmitter is intact.

He removes a small SILVER KEY hidden beneath the transmitter.
He pockets the key.

The case remains on the bench.


INT. ABANDONED TRAIN STATION — CONTINUOUS

Elena enters. Marcus turns.

ELENA
You stole the transmitter.

MARCUS
I stole the case.

He gestures toward the bench.
The BLUE SIGNAL CASE IS STILL THERE.

Elena approaches it.

ELENA
Why take it?

Marcus looks toward the dark tunnel.

MARCUS
Because someone else is looking for the key.

Elena notices the transmitter.

ELENA
The key isn't here.

Marcus says nothing.


EXT. TRAIN STATION — NIGHT

David arrives. He finds Elena outside.

DAVID
Where's Marcus?

ELENA
Gone.

DAVID
And the case?

Elena looks toward the station.

ELENA
Inside.


INT. ABANDONED TRAIN STATION — NIGHT

The station is empty.

The BLUE SIGNAL CASE IS GONE.

Elena searches the bench.
Only a torn piece of paper remains.

It reads:

"THE KEY OPENS THE LISTENER."


INT. MERIDIAN ARCHIVE — NIGHT

Elena returns to the archive.
She finds Marcus waiting in the records room.
He holds the SILVER KEY.

ELENA
You took it.

MARCUS
I had to.

ELENA
Where is the case?

Marcus looks confused.

MARCUS
You took the case.

Elena stares at him.

ELENA
No. You did.

Neither of them speaks.


INT. MERIDIAN ARCHIVE — CONTINUOUS

David enters carrying the Blue Signal Case.

Elena turns.

DAVID
Looking for this?

He places the case on the table.

ELENA
Where did you get it?

DAVID
It was in my office.

Elena looks at Marcus.
Marcus looks at David.

MARCUS
You moved it.

David says nothing.


INT. MERIDIAN ARCHIVE — LATER

The three examine the transmitter.
Elena reads the warning from her notebook.

ELENA
Never transmit after midnight.

Marcus shakes his head.

MARCUS
That's not a warning.

ELENA
What is it?

MARCUS
A rule.

He points to the transmitter.

MARCUS
The Listener only responds before midnight.

Elena checks the clock.
11:47 P.M.


INT. MERIDIAN ARCHIVE — 11:58 P.M.

Elena inserts the SILVER KEY into the transmitter.
Nothing happens.

DAVID
Maybe the key isn't for this.

Marcus notices a symbol beneath the desk.
He moves the Blue Signal Case.
A hidden panel is revealed.
Behind it is an old receiver.

Marcus connects the transmitter.
The receiver activates.

A voice crackles through the speaker.

VOICE (V.O.)
Elena Vale.

Elena goes still.

VOICE (V.O.)
If you are hearing this, your father failed.


INT. MERIDIAN ARCHIVE — CONTINUOUS

The voice continues.

VOICE (V.O.)
The Listener records every transmission.

Elena looks at the transmitter.

ELENA
Who is listening?

Static. Then:

VOICE (V.O.)
Someone who already knows where you are.

The lights go out.


INT. MERIDIAN ARCHIVE — MOMENTS LATER

Emergency lights flicker on.

The Blue Signal Case is open.
The SILVER KEY IS GONE.

Elena searches the room.
Marcus stands beside the receiver.
David is missing.

Elena looks at the dark doorway.

ELENA
David?

No answer.

Marcus looks at the clock.
11:59 P.M.

MARCUS
We have one minute.

CUT TO BLACK.

END
