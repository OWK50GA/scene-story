THE GLASSHOUSE

Written as a demo fixture for the Living Movie Memory system.
Universe: The Glasshouse
Project: The Glasshouse Stories (Canon Tier 1)
Story Unit: Film C — "The Glasshouse"
In-Universe Period: Present Day, 2026
In-Universe Date Start: 2026


INT. RIVERSIDE COMMUNITY THEATER — LOBBY — MORNING

An old theater being prepared for reopening. Dusty velvet curtains.
Stacks of folding chairs. Posters from productions decades past.

ELENA MARSH, 34, theater director, walks through the lobby carrying a
cardboard box of old programs. Behind her is NOAH BELL, 19, an
apprentice technician.

NOAH
Where do you want these?

ELENA
Archive room. Anything from before 2000 stays there.

Noah notices a small brass THEATER KEY hanging from Elena's belt.

NOAH
That the master key?

ELENA
One of them.

She removes it and unlocks a side door. The Theater Key is in Elena's
possession. It hangs from her belt.


INT. RIVERSIDE COMMUNITY THEATER — ARCHIVE ROOM — MORNING

Elena opens a cabinet. Inside is an old leather-bound PRODUCTION
JOURNAL. She runs her fingers across its cover.

ELENA
My mother used to keep this.

NOAH
She ran the theater?

ELENA
For twenty-two years.

Elena opens the journal. A handwritten note identifies an old stage
prop: THE SILVER CROWN — ORIGINAL 1978 PRODUCTION. The Silver Crown
was part of the theater's original inventory, created for the 1978
season. Its last known location according to the journal is
unspecified at this point.


INT. RIVERSIDE COMMUNITY THEATER — MAIN STAGE — AFTERNOON

Workers prepare the stage. A large wooden trunk sits near the wings.
Elena opens it.

Inside is the SILVER CROWN — tarnished but intact.

ELENA
There you are.

She lifts it carefully.

NOAH
You actually found it?

ELENA
My mother said it disappeared in 1981.

Elena carries the Silver Crown to a glass DISPLAY CASE near the stage
and places it inside. She locks the display case. The Silver Crown's
location is now the locked display case on the main stage. Elena
places the Theater Key back on her belt.


INT. RIVERSIDE COMMUNITY THEATER — OFFICE — EVENING

Elena studies the production journal. A 1981 entry reads: "The crown
was removed from the stage after the final performance. It will
remain in the theater archive."

Elena frowns.

NOAH
Problem?

ELENA
The story I heard was that it disappeared.

NOAH
Maybe whoever wrote this was wrong.

Elena closes the journal.

ELENA
Maybe.

The journal establishes an alternative account: the Silver Crown was
intentionally moved to the theater archive after 1981, not lost.


INT. RIVERSIDE COMMUNITY THEATER — MAIN STAGE — NIGHT

The theater is empty. Elena locks the display case a second time and
checks the lock twice. She removes the Theater Key from her belt and
places it on a HOOK beside the stage door. She leaves. The Theater
Key's location is now the hook beside the stage door. The Silver
Crown's location is the locked display case on the main stage.


INT. RIVERSIDE COMMUNITY THEATER — MAIN STAGE — NEXT MORNING

Noah enters. The display case is intact — no visible damage, still
locked. But the SILVER CROWN IS GONE from inside the case.

Noah calls Elena.

NOAH
Elena. The crown isn't here.

Elena rushes in and examines the case. No forced entry. No broken
glass. The lock is undamaged.

ELENA
Did you open this?

NOAH
No.

Elena looks toward the hook beside the stage door. The THEATER KEY
is also gone from the hook. There is no recorded event establishing
how either the crown left the locked case or how the key left the hook.


INT. RIVERSIDE COMMUNITY THEATER — BASEMENT — MORNING

Noah searches through storage. He finds the SILVER CROWN inside an
old costume trunk in the basement. Elena examines it. A tiny piece
of blue ribbon is caught beneath its edge.

ELENA
Why would someone move it down here?

NOAH
Maybe they didn't want it displayed.

The Silver Crown's location is now the basement costume trunk. No
transfer event has been recorded to explain how it left the locked
display case on the main stage.


INT. RIVERSIDE COMMUNITY THEATER — ARCHIVE ROOM — AFTERNOON

Elena returns to the production journal and finds another entry: "The
crown was placed in the basement costume trunk after the 1981 fire
drill. It was never returned to the stage."

NOAH
So it was here the whole time?

ELENA
Apparently.

This historical journal entry establishes that the crown's canonical
long-term location was the basement costume trunk. It does not explain
the present-day disappearance from the locked display case.


INT. RIVERSIDE COMMUNITY THEATER — OFFICE — EVENING

Elena speaks with MRS. VALE, 68, a former costume designer.

MRS. VALE
Your mother hated that crown.

ELENA
Why?

MRS. VALE
It was heavy. She kept moving it around.

Elena examines the blue ribbon.

MRS. VALE (CONT'D)
That ribbon belonged to your mother.

Mrs. Vale establishes that Elena's mother had a habit of moving the
Silver Crown to different locations. The ribbon connects the crown to
Elena's mother directly.


INT. RIVERSIDE COMMUNITY THEATER — MAIN STAGE — NIGHT

Elena carries the Silver Crown back upstairs and places it inside the
display case. She locks it. This time she keeps the Theater Key in
her pocket. The Theater Key's location is now Elena's pocket. The
Silver Crown's location is the locked display case on the main stage.

NOAH
Better?

ELENA
Much.

She looks at the crown through the glass.

ELENA (CONT'D)
Some things don't disappear.
They just get moved.

FADE OUT.

THE GLASSHOUSE — END
