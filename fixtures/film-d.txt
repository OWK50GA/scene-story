LOW WATER

Written as a demo fixture for the Living Movie Memory system.
Universe: The Tide House
Project: The Tide House Stories (Canon Tier 1)
Story Unit: Film D — "Low Water"
In-Universe Period: Present Day, 2026
In-Universe Date Start: 2026


EXT. NORTH COAST — TIDE HOUSE — MORNING

A weather-beaten house stands above a rocky shoreline. Grey water.
Stacked stone.

MAYA REED, 29, marine researcher, unloads equipment from a van. Her
younger brother SAM, 24, carries a waterproof case.

MAYA
Careful with that.

SAM
It's empty.

MAYA
It wasn't empty yesterday.

Sam opens it. Inside is a handheld SONAR UNIT — compact, ruggedized,
battery-powered. The sonar unit is in Sam's possession.


INT. TIDE HOUSE — KITCHEN — MORNING

Maya spreads tide charts across the kitchen table. A marked location
appears offshore: BLACK REEF.

SAM
You're still looking for it?

MAYA
The reef isn't the mystery.

She points to another marking on the chart.

MAYA (CONT'D)
This is.

A handwritten note reads: "Low tide reveals the entrance." Maya's
research goal is finding the submerged chamber beneath Black Reef.
The sonar unit's location is the kitchen table where Sam set it down.


EXT. SHORELINE — AFTERNOON

Maya and Sam walk the rocks at the inlet. Sam carries the sonar unit.
The tide is beginning to fall. Maya switches the sonar on. The screen
displays an unusual underwater structure below the inlet floor.

MAYA
There.

SAM
That's not natural.

Sam is carrying the sonar unit on the shoreline at the inlet. The
underwater structure's existence is now confirmed by sonar readings.


INT. TIDE HOUSE — STUDY — NIGHT

Maya examines an old CAPTAIN'S LOGBOOK found in the house. One entry
describes a submerged stone chamber beneath Black Reef. Another entry
mentions a BRASS COMPASS supposedly recovered from the chamber by a
previous occupant. Maya searches a desk drawer. The compass is not
there. The brass compass's location is unknown at this point.


EXT. SHORELINE — NEXT MORNING

Low tide. Maya and Sam return to the inlet. A narrow opening is
visible beneath the rocks where the water has pulled back. Sam enters
first. Maya follows. They are entering the submerged chamber together.


INT. SUBMERGED CHAMBER — DAY

Their lights reveal a stone chamber — old, hand-cut walls, a stone
ledge. On the ledge sits the BRASS COMPASS. Maya carefully picks it up.

MAYA
This is the one from the logbook.

Sam examines it.

SAM
Still works.

Maya puts the compass inside her waterproof EQUIPMENT BAG. The brass
compass's location is now Maya's equipment bag.


INT. TIDE HOUSE — KITCHEN — EVENING

Maya empties the equipment bag onto the kitchen table. The compass is
there. She places it beside the logbook on the table. The brass
compass's location is now the kitchen table beside the logbook.

SAM
We should photograph everything before we leave.

MAYA
Tomorrow.

She closes the logbook.


EXT. SHORELINE — NIGHT

A storm approaches. Maya and Sam watch waves break against the rocks.
Sam notices the sonar unit sitting on a dry stone ledge near the
water's edge.

SAM
You left this outside?

MAYA
I thought you had it.

They retrieve it. The sonar unit's location was the stone ledge on
the shoreline. There is no recorded event establishing how it moved
from inside the Tide House to the outside stone ledge. Maya and Sam
both deny placing it there.


INT. TIDE HOUSE — STUDY — MORNING

Maya studies the logbook again. She discovers a loose page hidden
inside the front cover. It reads: "The chamber cannot be reached at
high tide. The entrance is visible only for approximately forty
minutes at low water."

Maya checks the tide chart.

MAYA
Forty minutes.

SAM
Then we'd better be quick.

The logbook's loose page establishes the forty-minute access window
as a confirmed constraint on chamber access.


EXT. SHORELINE — LOW TIDE — AFTERNOON

Low tide again. Maya and Sam return to the inlet. The entrance is
visible. Maya carries the BRASS COMPASS. Sam carries the SONAR UNIT.
They enter the chamber. The brass compass's location is Maya's
possession as she enters the chamber.


INT. SUBMERGED CHAMBER — AFTERNOON

The chamber is darker than before. Maya shines her light across the
stone ledge. The compass is not on the ledge. She checks her
equipment bag. It is not there either.

SAM
Didn't you put it back here?

MAYA
No. I had it when we left this morning.

Maya's equipment bag is empty. The brass compass is missing from her
possession. No transfer event has been recorded for the compass
between the kitchen table in Scene 7 and Maya's possession in
Scene 10. There is no recorded event to explain its current absence.


EXT. SHORELINE — EVENING

They return toward the house. Sam carries the sonar unit. Maya carries
the logbook. No compass.

MAYA
Let's check the kitchen.


INT. TIDE HOUSE — KITCHEN — EVENING

The brass compass sits on the counter beside the sink. Maya stares at it.

SAM
Did you put it here?

MAYA
No.

She picks it up. The compass needle points toward the sea. The brass
compass's location is the kitchen counter. No transfer event explains
how it returned here from Maya's possession. Both Maya and Sam deny
moving it.


INT. TIDE HOUSE — STUDY — NIGHT

Maya reads the loose page again. On its reverse is one more sentence:
"The compass always returns to the house."

MAYA
(reading aloud)
The compass always returns to the house.

SAM
You think that's literal?

MAYA
I don't know.

She closes the logbook. The logbook explicitly establishes a rule
about the brass compass: it returns to the house. This is a
stated property of the compass within the story world, not an
inference. It may explain the unexplained location transition.


EXT. TIDE HOUSE — DAWN

Storm has passed. Maya stands on the porch holding the compass. Sam
joins her.

SAM
What happens now?

MAYA
We document everything.

She looks toward Black Reef.

MAYA (CONT'D)
Then we come back at low tide.

The brass compass's location is Maya's possession on the porch. The
sonar unit's location is inside the Tide House.

FADE OUT.

LOW WATER — END
